Here the split is: **A, B, C, D** on the A string; **E, F, G** and up on the E string. **A**
could be the open A string (as here) or the E string's low 3rd finger, and **E** could be
open E or the A string's 4th finger — pitch and staff position tell you which, context tells
you which is easier.

Notice **C** on the A string is a *low* second finger, and **F** and **G** on the E string are
low first and low second. Sharp keys will pull those up.
