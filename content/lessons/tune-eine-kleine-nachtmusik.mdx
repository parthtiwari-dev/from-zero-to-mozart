Mozart's *Eine kleine Nachtmusik* ("a little night music"), K.525, opens with one of the most
recognisable gestures in all of classical music — and it's built from exactly what this
movement has been about: **a I arpeggio answered by a V7 arpeggio.**

This is a **simplified** version of the first phrase, in first position — the pitch shapes are
Mozart's; the rhythm is smoothed out so you can hear the bones.

## The usual mistake

Playing the run heavy and grounded. It's a *rocket* — it should feel like it's taking off,
light and quick, the sound growing as it climbs. And the F♯ in phrase 2 is a leading tone:
lean it up, don't let it sag.
