A card fails. Now what? The wrong answer is to play it ten more times hoping it fixes itself.
The right answer is to work out **which layer broke** and fix only that.

## The four layers

A line has four things going on at once, and when it collapses, usually only *one* of them is
dragging the others down. Pull them apart:

| Layer | Isolate it by | You're checking |
|---|---|---|
| **Pitch** | **say the note names**, out loud, in order — *no rhythm* | did you read the notes? the key? the accidentals? |
| **Rhythm** | **clap / tap / count**, on one pitch or none, with the metronome | did the durations and the pulse hold? |
| **Left hand** | **finger the notes silently**, or **pizzicato slowly** — off the pulse | do the fingers know where to go? the string crossings? |
| **Bow** | **air-bow** the rhythm and the slurs — no string | is the bowing plan clear? the slur groups? |

Then **combine two at a time** — pitch + rhythm, then left hand + bow — then all four. One
recurring problem, one specific fix, and move on. Don't rehearse the whole line; repair the
break.

## Why isolating works on the violin especially

On the violin the bow and the left hand are doing completely different jobs at the same time —
the rhythm can even come from either one. When a line breaks, a beginner who replays it over
and over never learns *which hand* caused it. Splitting the layers makes the diagnosis obvious,
and then the fix is quick.

## Try it

<TryThis>
  Take a card that beat you. Instead of replaying it: say the pitches (no rhythm) — clean?
  Clap the rhythm (no pitches) — clean? Pizz it slowly — clean? Air-bow the slurs — clean?
  Whichever one *wasn't* clean is your problem. Fix that, combine it back, and leave the card.
</TryThis>

## The usual mistake

Replaying the whole line, full speed, again and again — which just grooves the mistake and
tells you nothing. The other mistake: trying to fix all four layers in one pass (say the
notes *and* count *and* watch the bow…) — you can't, and you end up fixing none. One layer at
a time.
