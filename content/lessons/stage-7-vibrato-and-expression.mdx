The daily vibrato and expression routine, gathered in one place. None of it is a piece — it's
the workout that makes expressive playing possible. Five focused minutes at the start of a
session, most days, for months. The sound of vibrato is the one thing this course can't play
for you, so much of this is "do it and listen to your own hand."

## How to use this page

Not all of it every day. **Always** the pulse ladder (item 1). Then **one** of items 2–4,
rotated. Two or three minutes total. **Stop the moment the hand tightens** — a tired hand
practises the wrong motion. Do the pulse for a minute at the start of every session even on
days you do nothing else here.
