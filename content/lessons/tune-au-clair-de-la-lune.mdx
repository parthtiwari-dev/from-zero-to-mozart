Possibly the simplest real tune there is — three notes for most of it, all steps, no leaps.
It's an 18th-century French song, and it was the very first sound ever recorded, in 1860.

## The one thing this tune is for

A **completely even bow** on repeated notes, and a clean skip (D up to F♯, missing E). The
song's middle section climbs to the A string — your first string crossing inside a tune — and
you can add it once these two lines are solid.

<Cite source="'Au clair de la lune' — French, 18th century, public domain">
  It's the standard first melody in French method books for the same reason we use it: the
  smallest possible set of notes, so the only thing to think about is the bow.
</Cite>
