An African-American spiritual, and a perfect Stage 2 tune: it opens with a **pickup**, leans
on a **dotted rhythm**, and holds long notes across bar lines. Only five notes — D, E, F♯, G,
A — all on the D and A strings, first position. In D major (two sharps).

## The one thing this tune is for

**Reading rhythm from the page.** It has almost every idea from this stage in one short tune —
a pickup, whole notes held through bar lines, a full bar of rest, and a dotted half. Count it
out loud, all the way through, before you play a note.
