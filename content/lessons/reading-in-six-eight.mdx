A compound-time reading check. Feel it **in two** — two dotted-quarter beats a bar — and let
the eighths flow between them. Count "1 2 3 4 5 6" while you learn each line, then just the
two big beats once it's under your fingers.

## If it comes out as 3/4

You're counting six beats instead of two. Put the metronome on just **two** clicks a bar and
fit three eighths between each. The click is a dotted quarter now, not a quarter.

<Cite source="6/8 as compound duple; Row Your Boat as the classroom example">
  The whole difficulty of 6/8 is trusting that the beat is the dotted note. Once your foot
  taps twice a bar, the rest follows.
</Cite>
