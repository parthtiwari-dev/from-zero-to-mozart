Before the left hand ever touches a string, the bow learns Twinkle. Suzuki students spend
weeks here — on the **open strings**, no fingers — drilling a clean start, an even stroke, and
a clean stop. This is where a good bow arm comes from.

## The one thing this is for

The **stopped bow** — starting the sound cleanly and stopping it cleanly, over and over.
Suzuki drills four rhythmic variations on these notes (the fast "Mississippi stop-stop" and
its cousins); you'll read those rhythms properly in Stage 2. For now, the plain version, and a
bow that never scratches.
