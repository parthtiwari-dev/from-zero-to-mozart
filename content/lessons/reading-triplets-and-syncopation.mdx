play, reveal. Keep the pulse rigid underneath both — that's the whole test.

## If either one collapses

For the triplet: split the beat into six even parts out loud and play every other one. For
the syncopation: clap the steady pulse with your foot and only the *notes* with your hands,
so you feel exactly where each one falls against the beat.
