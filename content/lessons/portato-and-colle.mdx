Two NEXT-level strokes that sit between the ones you already have.

## Portato

**Portato** (also called *louré*) is gently pulsed notes **under one bow** — the bow keeps
moving in one direction, but you give each note a soft swell and a slight separation, so it's
neither fully connected (legato) nor fully stopped (staccato). Written as **tenuto dashes under
a slur**.

## Collé

**Collé** ("glued") is a tiny, pinched stroke that **starts from the air**: the bow drops onto
the string, pinches a short note with the fingers, and lifts off again. It trains the
independence of the bow-hand fingers, which everything faster and bouncier later depends on.
There's no unique symbol — it's a practice stroke and a component of other techniques more
than something you'll see written.

Both are NEXT: worth touching once détaché, legato and a clean stopped stroke are comfortable.
