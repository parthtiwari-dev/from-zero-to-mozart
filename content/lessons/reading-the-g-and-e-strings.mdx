## The usual mistake

On the G string, panicking at the ledger lines and losing the high-2 B. On the E string,
playing F♯ and G♯ from habit when the notation — in C, G or D — may want F♮ and G♮. Check the
key before the finger drops.

  Play the G-string line, then the E-string line, back to back. Notice your eye travelling from
