Right after the clef, before the music starts, you'll often see one or more sharps (or flats).
That's the **key signature**. It's a standing instruction: *these notes are sharp (or flat)
every time, in every octave, for the whole piece* — unless a later mark cancels one.

That one sharp is **F sharp**, and a key signature with one sharp means **G major**. It's the
key of *Ode to Joy*, of the open strings ringing sympathetically, and of **Mozart's Violin
Concerto No. 3** — the piece at the end of this whole course. So an F written with no
accidental, in a piece with this key signature, is played as F sharp. Your ear and your second
finger both adjust once, at the start, and stay adjusted.

A key signature with **no** sharps or flats is **C major** (or A minor). We'll build the logic
of all of them in Stage 4.
