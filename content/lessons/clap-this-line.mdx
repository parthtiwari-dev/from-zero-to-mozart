Rhythm before the instrument — so here's a page with **no violin on it at all**. Each line is
a rhythm to **clap and count out loud** at the marked tempo, keeping the pulse dead steady.
No pitches to worry about; just the pattern in time.

Do them in order. A line passes when you can clap it twice through with the count running
continuously and no stumble on the subdivisions. If your own counting syllables are more
natural for the fast bits, use them — the clapping is what's being tested.

## If a line keeps failing

Strip it down further: clap it with **no counting**, just the pattern, very slow. Then add the
count back. Then bring the tempo up one notch at a time. A rhythm you can't clap, you
certainly can't play.

<Cite source="Leopold Mozart, 1756; Kodály — 'sound before symbol'">
  Both put rhythm in the voice and the hands before the eyes and the instrument. This page is
  that principle as a graded exercise.
</Cite>
