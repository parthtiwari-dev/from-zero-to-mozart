Every Suzuki student plays a piece called *Perpetual Motion* — an unbroken stream of even
notes, no rests, nothing to hide behind. It's the tune that proves you can hold a pulse
through *everything*. Here's a short one of our own, in G major, on the D and A strings.

The whole point: **the eighth notes stay dead even** from the first note to the last. Count
"1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &" out loud the whole way through.

## The one thing this tune is for

**Keeping the pulse through everything.** No rests to reset on, no long notes to relax into —
just an even eighth-note stream, so the only way through is a steady bow and a steady count.
If it speeds up, you'll hear it. Slow it right down until every eighth is the same length,
then bring the tempo up a notch at a time.
