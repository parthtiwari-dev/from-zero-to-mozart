The two numbers stacked right after the key signature are the **time signature**. The top
number says how many beats are in a bar; the bottom says what kind of note counts as one beat.

| Meter | Feel |
|---|---|
| 4/4 | four quarter-note beats per bar |
| 3/4 | three quarter-note beats per bar — waltz time |
| 2/4 | two quarter-note beats per bar — march time |
| 6/8 | six eighth-note subdivisions, usually felt as **two** big beats |

6/8 is the odd one out: six eighth notes are written, but they're usually felt as **two** big
compound beats (each made of three eighths), not six small ones — the difference between a
march and a jig. You'll meet that feel properly when we reach compound rhythms in repertoire;
for now, just recognise the shape "6/8" and know it doesn't count like 3/4 even though both add
up to six eighths.
