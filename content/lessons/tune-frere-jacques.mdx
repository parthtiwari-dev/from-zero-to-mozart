A French round, four two-bar phrases, each repeated. Once you can play it straight through,
play it *against the playback* — start four beats after the recording and you're singing a
canon with yourself.

## The one thing this tune is for

Playing it **as a round** against the recording — your first taste of holding your own line
while another is going. Get it solid alone first, then start a few beats late and don't let
the playback pull you off.

## The usual mistake

Speeding up or slowing to match the playback once you're rounding against it — the whole
point is holding *your* pulse independently. Count out loud. And letting the 4th finger land
short on "dor-mez vous" — reach for it and check it.
