Targets for `learning-a-tune-by-ear`. For each: **press play, listen with the violin down**,
then work it out by the six steps — audiate, rhythm, find home, degree by degree, correct,
join. **Reveal only to check** your finished version against the notation and the degree
numbers.

Work the ladder in order. Levels 1–2 are here as notated targets; Levels 3–4 are real
recordings you choose.

---

## Levels 3 & 4 — real recordings

No notation here — the point is to take these off an actual recording, by ear.

- **Level 3 — the first phrase of a hook you love.** Pick from the viral-melody list in
  `REPERTOIRE.md` (Hedwig's Theme, Married Life, He's a Pirate, Over the Rainbow, Skyrim…) —
  most open with a first-position-playable phrase. Just the first line. Slow the recording
  down; find home; take it degree by degree.
- **Level 4 — a whole short tune, played along with the track.** Learn it slowly and alone
  first, then follow `play-along`: match key, slow the track, lock the pulse, bring the speed
  up.

When you can do a Level 4 tune — hear a piece of music you like and play it back, in tune, in
time, on your own — you've reached the goal this whole course was pointed at.
