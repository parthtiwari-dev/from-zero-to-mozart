its string and finger, out loud — and only then check the answer.

The answer gives you name, string and finger under each note. The low ones (G, A, B) are all on
the **G string**; E and F are on the **D string**. The one that could go either way is **D** —
here it's the open D string, but it's also the G string's 4th finger.
