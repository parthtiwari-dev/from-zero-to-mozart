its string and finger, out loud — and only then check the answer.

The finger number is shown above each note in the answer; the string follows from the pitch.
The low notes (G, A, B) are all on the **G string**; E and F are on the **D string**. The one
that could go either way is **D** — here it's the open D string, but it's also the G string's
4th finger.
