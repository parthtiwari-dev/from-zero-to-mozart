**Legato** means smoothly connected — no gap, no re-attack between notes. On the violin, the
usual way to write "play these connected" is a **slur**: a curved line over a group of notes,
telling you to take them all **in one bow**.

The skill is bow distribution: four notes in one bow means each note gets about a quarter of
the bow, so plan where you start (near the frog for a down-bow slur) and pace it so you don't
run out of bow before the last note. The left hand does all the note-changing; the right arm
just keeps travelling at an even speed.

You met the slur symbol back in Stage 2 as "the curve over notes that move." Here it is as a
**bowing instruction** — and this is also the foundation for playing an entire phrase as one
musical breath, which is where Stage 7 picks it up.
