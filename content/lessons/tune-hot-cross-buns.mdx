Three notes, one string, going down. It's the first tune in a lot of method books because
there's nothing to it but the bow and three fingers — and it lives entirely on the G string,
so it's also your first real reading below the staff.

## The whole tune

The shape is **A – A – B – A**: "Hot cross buns" twice, the quick "one a penny" bar, then
"hot cross buns" to finish.

## The one thing this tune is for

The **high-2 B** on the G string. It's a *high* 2nd finger — up against where the 3rd finger
would go — not halfway between 1 and 3. Put it in the middle and you get B♭, and the tune
sours on the very first note.

## The usual mistake

The middle B♭ (2nd finger halfway) instead of B♮ (2nd finger forward, against the 3rd) — the
tune goes sour immediately. And rushing the "one a penny" eighths — they're exactly twice the
speed of the quarters, no faster; subdivide the bars before them.
