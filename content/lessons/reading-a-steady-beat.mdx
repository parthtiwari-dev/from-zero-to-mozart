Stage 2 is all about rhythm. This lesson is just enough of it to play the tunes at the end of
this stage — three note lengths and a count.

## Three lengths

| Note | Beats | Count it |
|---|---|---|
| **quarter note** (filled, with a stem) | 1 | "1" |
| **half note** (open, with a stem) | 2 | "1–2" |
| **whole note** (open, no stem) | 4 | "1–2–3–4" |

The **time signature** at the start — usually `4/4` — has a top number that tells you how many
beats are in each bar. In `4/4`, four. You count **1-2-3-4, 1-2-3-4**, steady, out loud or in
your head, and the notes fall on those numbers.

## Clap it before you bow it

Separate the two problems. **Clap and count the rhythm first**, with no violin — then the only
thing left to solve is the notes. This is Leopold Mozart's advice, word for word.

## The usual mistake

Playing the right notes with no pulse — reading without counting, so the long notes get
clipped and the short ones sprawl. The metronome is not optional here; start it slow.

<TryThis>
  Metronome at 60. Clap: four quarters, then two halves, then a whole, counting aloud each
  time. Then play the tune above, counting. If the count wobbles, slow the metronome down.
</TryThis>
