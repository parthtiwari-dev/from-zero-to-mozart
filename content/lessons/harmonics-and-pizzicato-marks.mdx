Two REFERENCE-level marks worth being able to recognise, even though playing them is well
ahead of where you are now.

## Harmonics

A small circle printed above a note usually means **natural harmonic**: instead of pressing the
string down to the fingerboard, you touch it very lightly at an exact point (a "node") and bow
normally — the string vibrates in segments and produces a high, glassy, flute-like pitch, quite
different from the note the fingering would normally give.

**Artificial harmonics** combine a normally-stopped note with a second finger touching lightly
a fourth higher — notated with a mix of a regular notehead and a small diamond-shaped one. Both
kinds are LATER material; recognise the symbol now, don't chase the technique yet.

## Left-hand pizzicato

A small **+** above a note means pluck it with a **left-hand** finger instead of bowing —
usually while the bow is busy holding a note on another string. Rare in beginner material, but
you'll see it in showier repertoire eventually.
