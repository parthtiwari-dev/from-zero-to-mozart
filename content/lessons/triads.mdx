Stack two 3rds on top of each other and you get a **triad** — the basic 3-note chord underneath
almost all Western harmony.

In the key of G major, stacking a triad on **every** scale degree gives you the key's whole
harmonic vocabulary — chords labelled by roman numeral: **I ii iii IV V vi vii°**. The three
that matter most day to day are **I, IV and V** (the "primary chords") — almost any simple
piece leans heavily on those three. **V** (built on D, the 5th degree) is special: it very
often carries a extra note that pulls hard back toward I — that chord, the **dominant 7th**,
is the sound of "this needs to resolve."
