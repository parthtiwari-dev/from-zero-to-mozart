REFERENCE — the hardest double-stop category, named so you know it exists and where to go
when you're ready for it. Not a performed skill in this course yet.

## What they are

**Fingered octaves** use 1st and 3rd finger (or another non-4th combination) instead of the
standard 1st/4th frame you just learned. **Tenths** — an octave plus a third — stretch even
further. Both demand excellent left-hand flexibility and precise, independent finger control
under a stretch that the regular octave frame doesn't ask for.

## Why they're REFERENCE here

This course's Intermediate-gate scope stops at the regular 1st/4th octave frame and stopped
thirds/sixths — genuinely useful, genuinely common technique. Fingered octaves and tenths
belong to a later stage of technical development, well past where this stage is aiming you.

## Where to go when you're ready

**Ševčík Op. 9** ("Preparatory Exercises in Double-Stopping") is the standard étude reference
for finger independence within a double-stop hand frame — it's already in this course's étude
ladder (`REPERTOIRE.md`). When regular octaves and thirds feel secure, that's where fingered
octaves and tenths are actually taught properly, with the slow, systematic drilling they need.
