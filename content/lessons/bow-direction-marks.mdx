Two small marks tell you which way the bow moves — useful when a bowing pattern really matters
for the sound or the phrasing.

| Mark | Name | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| ⊓ (a small square bracket) | down-bow | frog toward tip |
| V | up-bow | tip toward frog |

**No mark at all** usually just means: use whatever bowing you'd naturally alternate into, or
whatever your edition already established. Down-bow often (not always) lands on strong beats,
because a down-bow naturally has a touch more weight behind it at the frog.

You already know these — from Stage 0's *"the geography of the bow"* — this page is just their
written form.
