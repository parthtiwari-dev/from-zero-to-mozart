Your daily disposable stream. **One card a day**, at the level where it's *almost* easy.
Pre-read it (`the-pre-read`), play it **once, no stopping** (`the-never-stop-rule`), judge it
against the pass condition, then move on — don't replay, and don't come back to the same
card. When a whole level goes cleanly at a slow click for a week, move up.

**Pass condition, every card:** a steady slow pulse start to finish · at least 8/10 pitches
correct · no panic pause longer than one beat. *(Reveal each card's answer only after you've
played it.)*

## More cards

This is a starter set — one or two cards per level. The point is that each is played **once**
and never again, so the ladder is meant to be much longer. Re-notate any tune you know into a
new key and it becomes a fresh card; the ABRSM *Violin Sight-Reading Trainer* app and *Read
Ahead* generate endless graded material when you run out. (Levels 11+ — positions, key
changes — arrive with Stage 8b, after you can shift.)

  uses. The pass condition puts pulse and fluency first, note-accuracy second, on purpose.
