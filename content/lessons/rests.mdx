Silence is written too, and it has an exact length — the same value system as notes, just with
no pitch.

| Rest | Duration in 4/4 |
|---|---|
| whole rest | 4 beats |
| half rest | 2 beats |
| quarter rest | 1 beat |
| eighth rest | ½ beat |
| sixteenth rest | ¼ beat |

A rest is not "nothing to read." Your job during a rest is to **keep counting** and get ready
for what's next — lift the bow, shift a finger, breathe. Miss the rest's length and you'll come
in early or late on the note after it.
