A round, in **6/8** — so it rocks in two, like the boat. The whole tune is eight bars; once
it's steady, play it against the playback a bar behind, the way you'd sing it in a round.
In G major, first position, on the D, A and E strings.

Count it "**1** 2 3 **4** 5 6" while you learn each line, then feel just the two big beats.

## The one thing this tune is for

**Compound time.** Two beats a bar, each a dotted quarter, each dividing into three. Once
your foot taps twice a bar and the eighths flow between, 6/8 has clicked. Then play it as a
round against the playback — start a full bar after it and hold your own two-beat pulse.

<Cite source="American nursery round, first printed 1852 — public domain">
  The oldest surviving version is close to the one everyone sings now. It's built entirely on
  the G-major chord — G, B, D — which is why it rounds against itself so cleanly.
</Cite>
