A **scale** is an ordered set of pitches climbing (or descending) from a home note — the
**tonic** — back to itself an octave later. It's not just a warm-up exercise: a scale is the
**pitch vocabulary of a key**, the set of notes a piece in that key draws from most of the time.

Your G-major scale is **G A B C D E F♯ G**. That last G is the same note as the first, an
octave higher — the tonic returning "home."

Every major scale, in every key, has the **same internal shape** — the same pattern of close
and wide steps between neighbours. That pattern is what the next page unpacks, and it's why
"G major" and "D major" feel like the same kind of thing in a different place.
