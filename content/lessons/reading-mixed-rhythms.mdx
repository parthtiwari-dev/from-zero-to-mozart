The capstone reading check for Stage 2. Everything from the stage in one line — a tie, a
dotted figure, an eighth run, a rest. Long enough to feel like a real piece of music.

## The pre-read

Before the bow moves, in about twenty seconds:

1. **Time signature** — 4/4. Four beats a bar.
2. **Key** — no sharps or flats. All natural notes.
3. **Range** — D up to A, all on the D string.
4. **Rhythm traps** — the dotted figure in bar 2, the tie into bar 3, the rest in the last bar.
5. **Hear beat 1** in your head, then start.

## Where you should be

By the end of Stage 2 you can:

- read any note-value, dot, rest, tie and time signature by sight
- count any first-position rhythm out loud, continuously, in standard counting
- tell a beamed group from a double stop from a vibrato wobble
- feel simple against compound time, and read 6/8
- read a triplet and a simple syncopation without the pulse moving
- turn a note-value into an amount and speed of bow

If a line like this one still falls apart, it's almost always one layer — go back and drill
that one alone. Then the tunes in Movement I are next: the same skills, in real music.
