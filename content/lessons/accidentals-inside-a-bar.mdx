Sometimes a sharp, flat or natural appears in the middle of the music, attached to one note.
That's an **accidental**. It changes that note — and it keeps changing every note on the same
line or space **for the rest of that bar**. The bar line resets everything back to the key
signature.

So before you play a bar, **scan it**. A sharp early in the bar can still be in force on a note
later in the same bar that has no sign of its own. Miss that and you'll play a wrong note and
not know why.

The reading habit: **key signature first, then local accidentals, bar by bar.**
