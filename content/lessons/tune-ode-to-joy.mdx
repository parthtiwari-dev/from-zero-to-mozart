The tune this whole course is named for. Beethoven wrote it as the theme of the choral finale
of his Ninth Symphony — a melody deliberately simple enough for anyone to sing. It stays
inside a five-note range and never leaps.

## The one thing this tune is for

**Phrasing** — playing four notes as one thought, not four separate events. Sing the phrase
first. Where does it rise, where does it settle? Let the bow follow the voice.

## The usual mistake

Playing it as eight equal notes and flattening the dotted bar into evenness — the little
long–short lean on "wonder" (phrase 2) is the whole character. And clipping the held B and A
at the phrase ends: hold them the full two counts, counting through.
