F♯ — read by interval, land the high 2 on the D string.

## The usual mistake

Playing low 2 for F — the natural — where G major wants F♯, the high 2. In a one-sharp key,
your 2nd finger lives forward on the D string.

  Let your ear, not the label, tell you which one G major wants.
</TryThis>
