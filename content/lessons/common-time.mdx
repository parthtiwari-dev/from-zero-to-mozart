Instead of "4" over "4", you'll very often see a large **C** sitting where the time signature
should be. That's **common time** — it means exactly the same thing as 4/4, four quarter-note
beats per bar. It's everywhere in classical scores, Mozart's K.216 included.

You'll occasionally see a C with a vertical line through it — **cut time** (2/2), two half-note
beats per bar, twice as fast a feel. That's a REFERENCE-level detail for now; common time is the
one you'll meet constantly from here on.
