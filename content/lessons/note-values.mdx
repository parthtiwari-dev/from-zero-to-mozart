A note's shape tells you two things: a filled-in or open **head**, whether it has a **stem**,
and whether it has a **flag** or is joined to its neighbours by a **beam**. Together those tell
you how long the note lasts, counted in beats.

| Value | Name | Look |
|---|---|---|
| 4 beats | whole note | open head, no stem |
| 2 beats | half note | open head + stem |
| 1 beat | quarter note | filled head + stem |
| ½ beat | eighth note | filled head + stem + one flag (or beam) |
| ¼ beat | sixteenth note | filled head + stem + two flags (or beams) |

Press play and listen to the pulse stay identical while the notes speed up. That's the whole
idea: **duration is relative to a steady beat**, not an absolute length of time. Faster tempo,
shorter beats, same relationships.
