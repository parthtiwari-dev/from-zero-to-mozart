Marks that shape *how* a note starts and ends, not its pitch or its length on the page.

| Mark | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| dot above/below the note | staccato | shortened, separated |
| short dash above/below | tenuto | full value, a little weight |
| `>` above/below | accent | emphasise the attack |
| `^` above/below | marcato | strongly marked, more forceful than an accent |
| curved line over the note | fermata | hold longer than written — context and style decide how much |

Don't confuse the **staccato dot** with the **duration dot** from Stage 2 — same tiny mark,
completely different meaning, told apart only by where it sits: beside the notehead (duration)
versus above or below it (staccato).
