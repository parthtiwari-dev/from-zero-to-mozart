You know this tune from Stage 1 as plain even notes. Suzuki's whole first book uses it to
drill **articulation** — and Stage 3's job is reading that articulation off the page. Here is
Twinkle with a scheme on it: the bright "sparkle" phrases short and separated, the settling
answers smooth and joined.

In A major, first position, on the A and E strings. Shape: 1 – 2 – 3 – 4 – 1 – 2.

## The one thing this tune is for

**Reading an articulation scheme** — playing staccato where you see dots, legato under a slur,
tenuto under a line, and switching between them cleanly, all inside one tune you already know
by ear.

## The usual mistake

Letting every phrase drift back to the same "medium" touch — the whole point is the contrast
between them. And playing staccato *louder* to make it "more staccato": it's shorter, not
harder. Keep the bow small and let the silence do the work.
