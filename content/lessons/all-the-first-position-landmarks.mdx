## The four anchors, again

Open D, the clef's G, open A, open E. Know those four on sight and every other note is one or
two steps away.

## The usual mistake

Knowing each string on its own but freezing when a line jumps between them. The map is one
thing, not four — read by anchor and interval and the string takes care of itself.
