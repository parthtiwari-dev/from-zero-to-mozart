The last reading drill of this stage. It roams across all four strings and the full first-
position range — the open strings plus stopped notes above and below the staff. No key
signature, so every note is natural.

Bar one is the four open strings, low to high — you should recognise those on sight by now.
Bar two mixes registers: C on the A string, B down on the G string, G up on the E string, D on
the open D string. The wide jump from B (low, under the staff) to G (high, above it) is the
kind of leap that trips people — take a beat, find the landmark, then move.

If you can read this line cleanly and slowly, name every note, and land most of them in tune,
you've got what Stage 1 is for. The next stages add rhythm, then everything else on the page.
