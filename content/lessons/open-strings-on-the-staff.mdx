Your four open strings are four fixed points on the staff. You never have to work them out, and
every stopped note is a step or a skip from one of them.

## The usual mistake

Not using them. Reading a stopped note from scratch when an open string sits one step away and
would have handed you the answer.

<TryThis>
  Play each open string and find it on the staff — say "open A, second space" as you play it.
  Then pick any note near it and read it as "one step up from open A", and so on.
</TryThis>
