the rhythm**, then **say the note names in that rhythm**, then **air-bow it**, then **play** —
and only then reveal the answer.

## If it fell apart

Fix **one layer at a time**. Pitches right but rhythm collapsed? Put the violin down and clap
the rhythm alone against a metronome. Rhythm fine but the fingers hesitated? Go back to the
pitch-only D-string reading from Stage 1. Never fix both at once.

  "If pitch was correct but rhythm collapsed, isolate rhythm. If rhythm was correct but
  fingers hesitated, go back to pitch-only reading."
</Cite>
