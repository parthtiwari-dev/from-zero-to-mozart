Pitch and rhythm together now, on the D string you already know. Clap the rhythm first. Then
say the note names in that rhythm. Then air-bow it. Only then play — and only then check the
answer.

If the pitches were right but the rhythm fell apart, isolate rhythm alone — clap it against a
metronome with no violin at all. If the rhythm was right but your fingers hesitated, go back to
pitch-only reading on the D string page. Fix one layer at a time.
