Major and minor aren't the only scale families — REFERENCE for now, so a few names don't feel
alien if you hit them later.

| Family | Basic idea | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Chromatic | all 12 semitones in a row | NEXT/LATER |
| Major pentatonic | 5 notes drawn from a major scale | REFERENCE — useful in folk/pop |
| Minor pentatonic | 5 notes drawn from a minor scale | REFERENCE — useful in folk/pop |
| Blues scale | minor pentatonic + one extra "blue" note | REFERENCE |
| Modes (Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Aeolian, Locrian) | the major scale started from each of its own degrees | LATER — theory |
| Whole-tone | every step a whole step, no half steps | LATER |
| Diminished / octatonic | alternating whole and half steps | ADVANCED / REFERENCE |

There's no single finite list called "all scales" across every musical tradition — this is
just the standard Western tonal set most relevant to the repertoire ahead of you, including the
Vivaldi and Mozart you're building toward.
