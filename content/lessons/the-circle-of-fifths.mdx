A map of every major key and its key signature, arranged so neighbours are as closely related
as possible — each step clockwise adds one sharp; each step anticlockwise adds one flat.

<CircleOfFifths highlight="G" />

Start at **C** (no sharps, no flats) at the top. Move one step clockwise and you land on **G**
— one sharp, F♯, exactly the key you derived on the last page. Another step clockwise: **D**,
two sharps. The new sharp added at each step is always the 7th degree of the new key — that's
not a coincidence, it's the same "raise the note that doesn't fit" logic from the last page,
repeating.

Go anticlockwise from C instead and you pick up flats the same way: **F** (one flat), **B♭**
(two flats), and onward.

You don't need to memorise this circle today. Use it as a **map** you come back to — every time
you meet a new key signature, you can find it here and see immediately how closely related it
is to keys you already know.
