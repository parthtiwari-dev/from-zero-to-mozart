Marks that redirect where you play next, instead of describing a note.

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| repeat barlines (thick line + two dots) | play this section again |
| 1st / 2nd ending brackets | take the first ending the first time through, skip to the second on the repeat |
| D.C. (da capo) | go back to the very beginning |
| D.S. (dal segno) | go back to the sign |
| Fine | the actual end (often reached via a D.C. or D.S., not by playing straight through) |
| coda / "to coda" | jump ahead to the coda when instructed |
| rehearsal letters or numbers | location markers for talking about a spot — not notes |

The whole point of these marks is that the music you actually play is **longer than the page
you're reading** — a repeat sign or a D.C. sends your eyes backward while the sound keeps going
forward. Get used to scanning ahead for one of these before you start a new piece, so you're
not caught out mid-phrase.
