Nine positions on the staff — five lines, four spaces — running straight up the musical
alphabet A B C D E F G. You could memorise all nine. Better: memorise **four landmarks** and
read the rest as steps and skips from them.

## The four landmarks

All four are open strings or the clef's G — things already wired to your hand:

| Landmark | Staff position | On the violin |
|---|---|---|
| **open D** | just below the bottom line | D string, open |
| **the clef's G** | second line | D string, 3rd finger |
| **open A** | second space | A string, open |
| **open E** | top space | E string, open |

## Everything else is a step or a skip

A line and the space touching it are **one letter apart** — a step. A line and the next line
(or a space and the next space) are **two letters apart** — a skip.

## The usual mistake

Counting up from the bottom line every single time — "E, G, B, D…". It's slow, and it collapses
the moment you're on a ledger line. Anchor first, then step or skip.
