Here is every position on the treble staff, bottom to top, with its letter. This is the table
you're learning to not need.

| Position | Note |
|---|---|
| bottom line | E |
| 1st space | F |
| 2nd line | G |
| 2nd space | A |
| middle line | B |
| 3rd space | C |
| 4th line | D |
| 4th space | E |
| top line | F |

Notice it just runs through the alphabet, wrapping from G back to A. A line and the space above
it are always **one letter apart**; a line and the next line are **two letters apart** (a
"skip"). Reading fluently is mostly seeing those two shapes — step and skip — instantly, from a
landmark you already know.
