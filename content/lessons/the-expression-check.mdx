A **self-audit, not a gate** (`RULES.md` #4) — nothing here locks or unlocks anything. It's an
honest look at whether Stage 7's ground is solid before Stage 8b (reading in position) and the
Intermediate gate. One item on this list — *vibrate on a sustained note* — is a criterion of
that gate.

## What Stage 7 asked of you

**1 · Vibrate on a sustained note — and control it.** Not just "can you wobble," but: start it
from a straight note, stop it deliberately, widen it, narrow it. The switch, not just the
motion.

**2 · Vibrate on every finger and every string, evenly enough.** Especially the 4th finger and
the G and E strings — the ones it's tempting to skip.

**3 · Shape a phrase with time, not only with the bow.** An agogic lean on the right note; a
small rubato into a peak and back; a breath at the phrase end. The bow-shaping was Stage 5;
this is the layer on top.

**4 · Choose a tone colour for a character and produce it.** Play a phrase *sul tasto* for a
memory, near the bridge for an arrival — on purpose, not by accident.

**5 · Name a piece's character in one word, and make it audible.** "Lament." "March." Then
three concrete choices that a listener could hear.

**6 · Make about five expressive choices about a tune, and repeat them.** The same performance
three times running — deliberate, not improvised.

**7 · Play a Classical phrase in Classical style.** Narrow vibrato, little rubato, non-legato
default, contained dynamic — and still shaped, not dead.

## Where this leaves you

If most of that felt solid, you have the expressive layer — the thing that turns correct notes
into music, and the fifth of `PRD.md` §3's capabilities (**Choose**). Ahead: **Stage 8b** —
reading fluently in the positions Stage 6 opened — and then the **Intermediate gate**, whose
vibrato criterion you've just met.

Vibrato is the one skill in this course that most needs a real teacher's eye. If you can, have
an experienced player watch your hand once. A written description can't tell you whether your
thumb is quietly cheating.
