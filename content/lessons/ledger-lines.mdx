The staff only has room for about an octave and a half. For notes above or below it you draw
**ledger lines** — little extra lines, one per note as needed. Nothing new to learn: the same
step-and-skip rules just carry on past the edge.

## Below the staff — most of the G string

Middle **C** sits on the first ledger line below the staff. Keep going down and you're on the
**G string**: B just under middle C, A on the next little line, open G hanging below that. In
first position the whole G string reads below the staff — normal for the violin, not an
exception.

## Anchor from the edge

The trick with ledger lines: don't count from the middle of the staff. Count from the nearest
**open string** — open D for everything below, open E for everything above.

## The usual mistake

Freezing at ledger lines. They look like a different language and they aren't — B on the second
little line below is just "a skip under middle C", the same as any other skip.

<TryThis>
