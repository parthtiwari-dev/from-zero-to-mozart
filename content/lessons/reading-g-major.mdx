A reading drill in G major. Remember the key signature: every **F is F sharp**. Scan the line,
name every note, work out string and finger, *then* reveal the answer and play it slowly.

This is deliberately not a scale — you can't ride the shape of a tune you know. Every note is
a real decode: symbol → name → apply the key signature → string → finger.

The F is the interesting one: written with no sign, but F sharp because of the key signature,
and played as a **high 2 on the D string**. If your ear expected a plain F, that's the key
signature talking.
