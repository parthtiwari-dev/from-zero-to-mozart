## What "done" means

Reading all of this — naming the key, the degrees, the chord, the cadence, the phrase — is
**done**. That's the skill this stage builds, and it's real progress toward the whole point of
the course: opening an unfamiliar score and making sense of it.

*Hearing* all of it fluently by ear — that's an ongoing project, and Stage 8's ear gym is
where it gets its own dedicated training. *Playing* chords, arpeggios in tempo, minor keys in
tune with expression — that's Stages 5, 6 and 7. Keep those separate in your mind. You don't
have to hear a deceptive cadence instantly yet; you have to know what one *is* and be able to
find it on the page.
