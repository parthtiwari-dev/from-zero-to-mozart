## If it comes out even

Slow right down and exaggerate: hold the long note a fraction longer, clip the short one a
fraction shorter — then settle it back to exact. Say the count with the "&" in it out loud so
the eighth lands on the off-beat, not on the next beat.

  "The pitches here aren't the hard part. The dotted timing is what's being tested. If it comes
  out even instead of long–short, slow the tempo down and tap before you bow."
