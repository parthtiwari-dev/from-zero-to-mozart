interval patterns, different key signatures, different feel.

Compare that to G major's **G A B C D E F♯ G** from a few pages back. Three notes are
different: B is flattened to B♭, E to E♭, and F♯ drops to plain F. That's why G minor's key
signature (two flats) looks nothing like G major's (one sharp) even though both start on G.

**G major and G minor are *parallel* keys** — same tonic, different mode. That's a different
relationship from the one on the next page.
