How loud or soft — written as lowercase letters (from Italian *piano* = soft, *forte* = loud)
below the staff.

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| pp, p | soft to very soft |
| mp | moderately soft |
| mf | moderately loud |
| f, ff | loud to very loud |
| cresc. / hairpin opening | growing louder |
| dim. or decresc. / hairpin closing | growing softer |
| sf, sfz | a sudden accent on one note |
| subito | suddenly (e.g. "subito p" — suddenly soft) |

A **hairpin** — two lines meeting at a point — is the graphic version of crescendo/decrescendo:
opening outward means grow louder, closing inward means grow softer, over exactly the notes it
stretches across.
