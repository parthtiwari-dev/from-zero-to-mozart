## The one thing this tune is for

**Stepwise reading on one string** — line, space, line, space — and keeping the bow even while
the fingers walk underneath it. Nothing here leaps; it's all steps and one small skip.

## The usual mistake

The bow bumping every time a finger drops or lifts — the walk should sound smooth, the bow
never noticing. And the high-2 B played in the middle (B♭): keep the 2nd finger forward,
against the 3rd, all the way through.
