Stage 2 gave you the repeat barline and the first-and-second endings. This lesson puts them
together — reading a **route** through a whole piece — and shows what all that repetition is
telling you about how the music is built.

## A quick recall

- **`‖:` … `:‖`** — the dots always face the music you go back and play. Play the enclosed
  bars **twice**, then carry on.
- **No opening `‖:`** — the `:‖` sends you all the way back to the **top of the piece**.
- **`1.` `2.` `3.` brackets** — different endings for the repeated section (*volta*, "time":
  *prima volta*, *seconda volta*). Play the `1.` bracket the first time, skip it and take `2.`
  the second time, and so on.

## The usual mistake

Playing both endings in a row the first time through. Missing that a lone `:‖` with no
opening partner sends you back to bar 1 of the whole piece, not just a few bars. And losing
your place on the second pass through a volta — the fix is always to trace it first.
