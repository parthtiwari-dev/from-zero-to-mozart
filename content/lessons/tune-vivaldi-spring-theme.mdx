Vivaldi's *Spring* — the first of *The Four Seasons* — opens with a theme so famous you've
almost certainly heard it. It's a **ritornello**: a tune that keeps coming back between the
solo episodes, like a chorus between verses. This is a **simplified** version of the opening
idea, moved to **D major** for the first-position hand (the original is in E major).

## The echo — f then p

## The usual mistake

Playing the repeated notes all joined together and mushy. They should be **distinct and
buoyant** — a little space between each, a spring in the bow. And the echo is a *volume*
change, not a *tempo* change: keep the pulse rock-steady.
