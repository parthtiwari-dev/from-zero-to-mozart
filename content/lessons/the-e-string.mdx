The E string, natural notes, first position. This one's a little different — its low notes need
a *low first finger*.

| Note | Finger | Where |
|---|---|---|
| E | 0 | open |
| F | 1 (low) | right up near the nut |
| G | 2 (low) | close to the 1st finger |
| A | 3 | |
| B | 4 | |

**F natural** on the E string is a *low* first finger — pulled back close to the nut, closer to
the head than feels natural at first. **G natural** is a low 2. In sharp keys these both slide
up: F sharp is a normal 1, G sharp a normal 2.

Above the staff the ledger lines come back: **A** is on the first ledger line above, **B** in
the space above that. Same counting rule as below — F is the top line, so G is the space above,
A the ledger line, B the space above.
