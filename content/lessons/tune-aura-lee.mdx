*Aura Lee* is an American song from 1861 — you'll know the tune as *Love Me Tender*, which
borrowed it word for word a century later. It's gentle, it's in **C major**, and it's the
tune that finally gives your **C-major first-position hand** a real workout.

## Why C major is its own challenge

C major has no sharps — but on the violin it uses the **low 2nd finger** on both the D and A
strings (for F and for the top C), and it starts with the **3rd finger** on the G string.
There are no open strings on the way up as anchors. Your ear does more work here than in G or
D.

## The usual mistake

Playing the low 2nd finger (F, and the top C) too high — drifting toward F♯ and C♯ out of the
D-major habit your hand has built. In C major those fingers pull *back* to touch the 1st
finger. Check them against your ear: F should sound a half step above E, not a whole step.
