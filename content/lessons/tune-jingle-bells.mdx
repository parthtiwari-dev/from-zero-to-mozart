## The one thing this tune is for

**Repeated notes with a still left hand.** The finger stays down; only the bow moves. If the
pitch wavers on the repeats, you're squeezing and releasing — let the finger rest.

<Cite source="'Jingle Bells' — James Lord Pierpont (1857), public domain">
  Written for a Thanksgiving service in Massachusetts, originally called "One Horse Open
  Sleigh". Ours is in D major, first position, rhythm simplified to whole beats.
</Cite>
