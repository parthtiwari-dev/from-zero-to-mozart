Small decorations added to a note or between two notes — the melodic equivalent of an
articulation mark, but affecting pitch rather than attack.

| Mark | Name | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| tr (sometimes with a wavy line) | trill | rapid alternation with the note above |
| a small "s" curve | turn | a quick 4-note pattern around the main note |
| a small zigzag | mordent | one quick flick to a neighbour note and back |
| a tiny note before the main one | grace note | a quick decoration leading into the main note |

Don't improvise an ornament's exact execution just from the symbol — trills, turns and mordents
were realised differently in different periods (Baroque vs. Classical especially), and a
teacher or a stylistically-informed recording is the real guide. For now: **recognise the
symbol and know its family** — the next page goes one layer deeper on how they're actually
played.
