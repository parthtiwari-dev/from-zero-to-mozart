## The one thing this tune is for

Reading a line by **step and skip** while your ear insists it already knows the tune. Trust
the page: name each note, find its string, then play. It's a short line — use it to practise
looking.

## The usual mistake

Letting the familiar tune run ahead of your eye — playing from memory and drifting off the
written notes. Read it a note at a time, out loud, the first few times through. And scraping
the string crossings between the D and A strings: lead each one with the whole arm, slowly.
