The tune this whole tradition starts on. Shinichi Suzuki built his entire first book around
it, and for good reason: six notes, a shape you already carry in your ear, and every bowing
problem in miniature.

## The one thing this tune is for

The bow. Before the left hand ever joins, Suzuki has students play the Twinkle *rhythms* —
short-short-long-long and its cousins — on open strings, drilling a clean start, a stopped
bow, and an even stroke. Everything after Twinkle assumes those are in place.

<Cite source="the melody is 'Ah! vous dirai-je, maman' (French, 18th century) — public domain">
  Mozart wrote a famous set of variations on the same tune (K.265). Suzuki chose it because a
  child already carries the melody — so the only new problem is the instrument.
</Cite>
