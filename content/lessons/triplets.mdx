A small **3** bracketed over a group of notes means: squeeze this many notes into the time a
different number would normally take. A **triplet** is the common case — three notes in the
space of two.

Triplets and their relatives (duplets, quintuplets — REFERENCE for now) are called **tuplets**.
The bracketed number is the only clue on the page that something irregular is happening to the
beat; without it, three notes would just be a different, unrelated rhythm.
