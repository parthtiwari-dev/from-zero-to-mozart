Time to put the eye and the hand together. For every natural note on the A and D strings — the
first two you learned — you want the chain **staff position → pitch → string and finger** to
happen in one glance.

## The usual mistake

Reading name → finger and skipping the pitch. You decode the symbol, place the finger, and
never actually *hear* what should come out. Sing the note, then play it, then check.

<TryThis>
  Play the A-string line above once, reading only the labels. Then hide them and do it again
  from the notation alone. The second time is the one that counts.
</TryThis>
