A small dot **after** a notehead adds **half of that note's original value** on top.

- A dotted **half** = 2 beats + 1 = **3 beats**.
- A dotted **quarter** = 1 beat + ½ = **1½ beats**.

That's it — one rule, applied to any value.

Don't confuse this dot with the small dot printed directly **above or below** a note — that one
means *staccato* (short and separated) and has nothing to do with duration. Same little mark,
completely different job, told apart only by where it sits.
