# Stage 0–2 audit (2026-09-02)

Every published lesson in Stages 0, 1 and 2 checked against the bar in `docs/PRD.md` §7 /
`docs/CONTENT-STANDARD.md` §7:

1. opens with the plain-language idea (no jargon first)
2. walks the eye across the *actual* notation
3. says what to listen for
4. one concrete thing to try on the violin
5. names the usual beginner mistake + the fix
6. one attributed lineage `<Cite>` (concept + annotated lessons)
7. **2–4 play-around examples** for a concept/skill lesson (`RULES.md` #5)
8. every pitched example labels every note name + string + finger (`RULES.md` #1)
9. every stage ends at **complete** real music with a per-line how-to-play note (R2, R3)
10. still passes the hands-free test

`PASS` = meets the bar. `NEEDS-WORK` = one or two concrete gaps, listed. Nothing here is a
rewrite; the structure and voice are sound throughout — the gaps are **play-around count** and
**tune completeness**.
