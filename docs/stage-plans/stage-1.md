# Stage 1 — First position & reading the staff: research + plan

**For approval before writing.** This is a *rebuild*, not a touch-up — the current 23 Stage-1
lessons are thin, use un-taught rhythm notation, and end at nothing you can play. Stage 1 is
where the course either becomes real or stays a skeleton.

Read `docs/THE-PATH.md` first. Stage 1's job: **you can read any first-position natural note
off the staff, know its string and finger, land it roughly in tune — and play ~12–18 real
tunes.**

---

## What's wrong with Stage 1 now

1. **Thin.** Most lessons state a fact + one 4-note fragment. No skill is drilled.
2. **Rhythm-notation gap** (`NOTATION-COVERAGE.md`): the reading drills show a common-time
   signature and whole / half notes — rhythm not taught until Stage 2. Stage 1 is about
   *pitch*.
3. **No end goal.** Nothing to play. No tunes exist anywhere in the course yet.
4. **Missing skills.** No *intervallic reading* (steps / skips / landmarks — the thing that
   actually makes reading fast). No *finger-pattern* lesson (the four first-position hand
   shapes). Left-hand *action* (dropping / keeping fingers down) barely touched.

## The research that shapes the rebuild

- **Read intervals, not note-names.** The consensus beginner method: memorise a handful of
  **landmark notes**, then read everything else as **steps and skips** up or down from them.
  Naming every note is slower and blocks musical thinking. → new lesson: *Steps and skips*.
  *(Sources: musicandtheory.com, colourfulkeys.ie, colorinmypiano.com, teachsuzuki.)*
- **The four first-position finger patterns** — the hand shapes every first-position key is
  built from: **1–23–4** (major), **12–3–4** (natural minor), **1–2–34** (high 3), **1–2–3–4**
  (whole-tone). → new lesson: *The four finger patterns*.
  *(Sources: violinspiration "finger positions", violinlounge, Doflein Book 1, Simon Fischer.)*
- **Why beginner violin lives in G / D / A** — the open strings are a stack of fifths, so
  those keys fall straight under the first-position hand (already in `why-g-major-has-one-sharp`;
  Stage 1 leans on it). Suzuki's Book 1 is in **A major** for exactly this reason — hence the
  Twinkle-in-A tune set.
- **Rhythm-lite for Stage 1:** the tunes need *some* rhythm. Stage 1 teaches only **quarter,
  half and whole notes counted against a steady beat** (a single short lesson), with a pointer
  that the full rhythm system is Stage 2. Pure *pitch* drills carry **no metre** (`M:none`,
  stemless) so no un-taught symbol appears.

## The rebuilt Stage 1 — six movements

Each lesson: the six beats, 2–4 play-around exercises (not one fragment), every pitched note
labelled name/string/finger. Tunes drop the moment their notes are covered.

### Movement A — The hand on the string (left-hand geography)

| # | Lesson | Job |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | `four-strings-four-fingers` | the 4 strings under the hand; fingers 0–4 are **fingers, not pitches**, always paired with a string; the elbow swings under for the low strings |
| A2 | `the-four-finger-patterns` *(new)* | the four first-position hand shapes (1–23–4 / 12–3–4 / 1–2–34 / 1–2–3–4); feel the whole-step and half-step gaps; drill each pattern on one string, silently then bowed |
| A3 | `low-and-high-fingers` | low 1 / 1, low 2 / high 2, 3 / high 3 — the semitone that moves; how a pattern changes when you slide one finger |
| A4 | `keeping-fingers-down` *(new)* | drop from the knuckle onto the tip; **keep the fingers behind a note down** ("blocked" fingers); lift cleanly; the finger stays over its note |
| A5–A8 | `the-{g,d,a,e}-string` | every natural note on each string, note by note — name, finger, where it sits, the tuning check; **2–3 patterns per string** + the same-pitch check |
| A9 | `one-pitch-more-than-one-place` | same written pitch, more than one fingering; the verified open-string checks (G-str 4 = open D, etc.); the "contextual, here's the default" habit |

### Movement B — The page (reading pitch)

| # | Lesson | Job |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | `the-staff` | five lines, four spaces; pitch = height, up = higher |
| B2 | `the-treble-clef` | the G clef and why it curls round the G line; the violin's home clef |
| B3 | `lines-and-spaces` | naming lines and spaces; **and the landmark notes** — treble G (the clef line), plus open D, open A, open E on the staff |
| B4 | `steps-and-skips` *(new)* | read by **interval**: a step = line→space; a skip = line→line / space→space; up vs down. Drill: read a line as "step up, step up, skip down…" *before* naming a single note |
| B5 | `ledger-lines` | the short lines above and below; the whole G string reads *below* the staff |
| B6 | `open-strings-on-the-staff` | the 4 open strings as your anchors on the page |
| B7 | `reading-the-g-and-d-strings` | connect finger → pitch → **staff position** for G and D; a *pitch-only* drill (no metre) + reveal |
| B8 | `reading-the-a-and-e-strings` | same for A and E |
| B9 | `which-note-is-this` (was `which-b-is-this`) | the register problem — same letter, different octave; C4 vs C5 as *later* language; how to tell from the staff |
| B10 | `all-the-first-position-landmarks` | the full first-position pitch map on the page; a mixed pitch-only drill + reveal |

### Movement C — Sharps, flats & the key signature

| # | Lesson | Job |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | `sharps-flats-and-naturals` | ♯ raises, ♭ lowers, ♮ cancels — and **which finger moves** (the low↔high finger, back to Movement A) |
| C2 | `the-key-signature` | the ♯ or ♭ after the clef; it applies all bar, every octave; the order of sharps (F C G D A E B) |
| C3 | `accidentals-inside-a-bar` | an accidental lasts to the bar-line, then resets; courtesy accidentals |
| C4 | `reading-in-g-major` | one sharp (F♯); read a *pitch-only* G-major line + reveal; this is where Movement A's "high 2 on the D string" and Movement B's reading meet |
| C5 | `reading-in-d-and-a` | two and three sharps; the pattern is the same, the hand shape shifts |

### Movement D — Landing it in tune (the ear — this user's growth axis)

| # | Lesson | Job |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | `landing-in-tune` | knowing the finger ≠ playing in tune; check against the open string; the drone |
| D2 | `ringing-tones` *(new)* | notes that set an open string ringing (D on the A string, A on the E string, etc.) — a free in-tune check built into the violin; Galamian / Fischer on sympathetic resonance |
| D3 | `sa-and-the-letter-names` | movable **Sa** (tonic as a function) ↔ fixed letter names; movable-do solfège as the bridge — built for this user's tabla background |
| D4 | `hearing-a-wrong-note` *(new)* | the ear catching itself: too high / too low, and the tiny finger move to fix it; slow, drone-backed |

### Movement E — Taking the scaffolding off

| # | Lesson | Job |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | `reading-a-steady-beat` *(new, rhythm-lite)* | quarter / half / whole notes counted 1-2-3-4 against the metronome — *just* enough to play the tunes; full rhythm is Stage 2 (pointer) |
| E2 | `taking-the-help-away` | the scaffold fades one cue at a time: name+string+finger → name+finger → name → plain notation; you choose your level |
| E3 | `the-first-position-reading-check` (checkpoint) | a mixed line, plain notation, self-check; "you can read first position" |

### Movement F — The tunes (the end goal, released across the stage)

The **Stage 1 piece set** — first position, quarter/half/whole notes only, all authored by us
as annotated lines with **per-line how-to-play notes** (bow placement, direction, part of bow,
weight, what to listen for). Also on `/practice`.

| Tune | Key / strings | Unlocks after | Teaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hot Cross Buns** | G string (B A G) | A5 `the-g-string` | three notes, one string, one bow per note |
| **Merrily We Roll Along** / **Mary Had a Little Lamb** | G string (G–D range) | A5 | stepwise motion on one string |
| **Ode to Joy** (phrase 1 → whole) | G major, G string | A5 + C4 | the course's own tune; F♯ where the D appears higher |
| **Au Clair de la Lune** | G + D strings | A6 | a first string crossing in a tune |
| **Frère Jacques** (round) | G major | A6 + C4 | a round — play against the playback |
| **Twinkle Twinkle** (theme) | A major (Suzuki) — A + E strings | A7 + A8 | the Suzuki starting tune; 0–1–2–3 on the A string |
| **Twinkle** — one variation | A major | + E1 | the same tune, a rhythm you'll meet properly in Stage 2 (pointer) |
| **Lightly Row** | A major | A8 | Suzuki #2; skips |
| **Song of the Wind** | A major | A8 | Suzuki #3; staccato feel, energy |
| **Go Tell Aunt Rhody** | A major | A8 | Suzuki #4; phrasing, a held last note |
| **French Folk Song** | D major | C5 | minor-tinged colour; slur-free legato line |
| **Long, Long Ago** | D major | C5 | a singing line, whole bows |
| **When the Saints Go Marching In** | D major, D + A | C5 | a skip up to start; a confident tune |
| **London Bridge** / **Baa Baa Black Sheep** | G or D major | A6 | short, very early, pure step/skip reading |
| **Jingle Bells** (chorus) | D major | C5 + E1 | repeated notes, a familiar target |
